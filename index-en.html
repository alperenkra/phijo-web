PhiJo is a free iPhone app that shows one quote a day. If you opened it today, you'd see the one above.

Widget

It goes on your home screen and lock screen. The quote changes on its own every day, even if you don't open the app. Your streak shows in the bottom corner.

You can show a quote picked for you instead of the daily one. With Premium, your own quotes too.

Feed

Below today's quote there's one picked for you, and below that, a feed that keeps going. It's ordered by what you read and what you skip. Narrow it to a theme or to the authors you follow.

10 quotes a day on the free version, unlimited with Premium.

Streak

The days you open the app are marked on a calendar, and your streak grows. If you miss a day, a freeze protects it. The streak board lets you compare with others.

23

Journal

Write a note on a quote and it sits on top of that quote on the home screen, like a sticky note. Press and hold to drag it where you want. When the quote comes around again, your note is still there.

All your notes are listed by date in the Journal. You can lock it with Face ID or a PIN.

Eight types

When you first open the app it asks eight questions and places you in one of eight philosophical types. Your "for you" quotes start from there, then change with what you like.

The original

The quotes are picked by hand, and we try to leave out the unsourced ones that get passed around online. As of October 2026 there are 287, and 226 of them come with the text in the language it was written in.
