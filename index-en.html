What is it?

i.

One quote a day

A curated quote each day. No endless feed; just a single thought to open the day with.

ii.

A quiet journal

Write notes on quotes, stick them like paper sticky notes. Your thoughts stay anchored to the quote.

iii.

For you

Your onboarding choices and in-app behavior shape a personal daily recommendation.

8 philosophical types

A short 7-round quiz reveals your philosophical type. Recommendations follow your type.

Stoic Sage

Holds wisdom, virtue, and patience above the pull of external things.

Existentialist

Questions meaning, freedom, and the solitude of being human.

Romantic

Reflects on love and the passing of time; keeps the heart at the center.

Brave Soul

Sees courage and freedom as the essence of life; ready to take risk.

Contemplative

Quietly meditates on solitude, mortality, and wisdom; seeks inner stillness.

Mystic

Believes in a universal connection through love, meaning, and wisdom.

Pragmatist

Turns patience, courage, and time into action; constructive and resilient.

Lyrical Thinker

Watches the poetic dance of love, death, and time; sees life as an aesthetic whole.
