Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 20, 2026

In the app

Fastest way: in PhiJo go to Profile → Account → Delete account. Your account and everything linked to it is deleted immediately.

By email

If you can't access the app, email phijo.app@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request". Write from the email address linked to your account and we will find and delete it within 30 days and let you know.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its linked email / Apple / Google sign-in
  • Your favorites, follows, journal notes and own quotes
  • Your profile quiz answers and birth date
  • Your preferences, interaction signals, usage records and push token
  • Feedback and error logs you sent

Purchase records may be kept by the App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat where the law requires it. A Premium subscription has to be cancelled separately in the store.
