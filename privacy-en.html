Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

PhiJo ("the app", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, where it is stored and what your rights are. It applies to both the iOS and Android versions.

PhiJo is a minimalist app made by an independent developer. We show no ads and use no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

1. Data We Collect

Your account

  • Anonymous user ID — the app gives you a random ID on first launch. No name, phone number or email is required.
  • Email address — only if you choose. When you sign in with email, Apple or Google it is linked to your account so you can open it on another device. We never store passwords.

What you create in the app

  • Your favorite quotes and the authors and themes you follow
  • Your journal notes and the quotes you add yourself
  • Profile quiz: your optional birth date and your answers to the personality questions (to work out your philosophical type and your personal quote suggestions)
  • Preferences such as theme, language, font and notification times
  • Messages you send from the feedback screen and an optional reply email

Usage and diagnostics

  • Interaction signals — which quotes you like, write notes on, skip or linger on in the feed. Used to personalize your suggestions.
  • Coarse usage counts — app sessions and which screens were opened (not what you read on them). Used to understand which features are used; written only to our own database.
  • Error logs — if the app hits an error: the error message, app version, platform, OS version and language.
  • Push token — if you allow notifications, a device-specific push token so we can announce new versions.

Purchases

  • If you buy PhiJo Premium, payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details. Your subscription status is processed by RevenueCat together with your user ID.
  • iOS only: if you installed the app from an Apple Search Ads ad, Apple's AdServices attribution token is passed to RevenueCat. This token is not the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not require tracking permission.

Stays on your device only

  • The journal lock PIN and biometric check (Face ID, fingerprint) — never sent to a server.
  • Quote and journal reminders are scheduled on the device.
  • The quotes your widget shows are cached on the device.

What we don't collect

  • Location
  • Camera, microphone or photo library
  • Contacts
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, Android advertising ID)
  • Cross-app or cross-site tracking data

2. How We Use Data

  • Core features: favorites, journal, profile, sync across devices
  • Personal quote suggestions and your personal feed (computed on our server from your signals)
  • Unlocking Premium features
  • New version announcements
  • Fixing errors and understanding which features are used

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing lists or sale to third parties.

3. Storage and Location

Account data is stored on Supabase Inc. infrastructure in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) and encrypted in transit with HTTPS. It is covered by the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK.

Data is kept for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account deletes everything linked to it (see section 6).

4. Service Providers

These services process data only on our behalf and only for the task listed:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, storage (privacy)
  • RevenueCat — purchases and subscription status (privacy)
  • Expo (650 Industries) — delivering push notifications (privacy)
  • Apple and Google — app distribution, payments, notification delivery and (if you choose) Sign in with Apple / Google
  • Google Gmail — sending sign-in verification codes by email

We share data with no other third party.

5. Your Rights (GDPR + KVKK)

You have the right to:

  • Access and correct your personal data
  • Request deletion
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability (you can export your journal from within the app)

Requests: phijo.app@gmail.com
We reply within 30 days.

6. Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account in the app: Profile → Account → Delete account. Deletion cannot be undone; your favorites, journal notes, own quotes, profile answers, preferences, feedback and usage records are permanently deleted. Premium subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play; deleting your account does not cancel them.

No access to the app? Request account deletion.

7. Children

PhiJo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe we have, contact us and the data will be deleted promptly.

8. Cookies

The app uses no cookies. This website has no analytics, cookies or trackers either.

9. Changes

We update this policy when needed and announce significant changes in the app and through the "Last updated" date on this page.
